\subsection{Separability and transfer (RQ2)}
\todo{Your strongest negative result -- state it plainly. DAN reduced

\subsection{Conditional versus marginal alignment (RQ3)}
\todo{DAN and DANN match the marginal $P(f)$; CDAN matches the conditional

person $+23.13$ against horse $-33.95$ and elephant $-15.68$, net $-6.41$.

semantic structure.}

\subsection{Invariance and discriminability (RQ2)}
\todo{DAN-DG at $\lambda=1$ produced separability 54.67 against a 33.3 chance
